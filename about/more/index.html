More about me

I’m a third-year computer science student at Kuang Yaming Honors School, Nanjing University.

Beyond technical work, I immerse myself in economics, philosophy, political science, psychology, and literature. I have a deep passion for Buddhist philosophy and Stoicism, and I actively practice meditation to cultivate clarity and resilience.

Music and sports are my constant companions. I enjoy listening to virtually every genre — from rap and jazz to classic Cantonese songs, R&B, rock, and more. I’m also a dedicated runner and calisthenics enthusiast.

The quote You only live once strongly resonates with me. Though it’s sometimes hard to tell whether we live to die or die to live, I believe life is a rewarding and beautiful journey worth embracing fully.

This site is a record of that journey — my pursuit of knowledge, growth, and meaningful impact.
